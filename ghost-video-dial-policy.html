Privacy Policy

Ghost Video Dial (formerly On the Sofa for YouTube)
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Price, the one-year license, repurchase, and refunds are in Terms of Sale. This Privacy Policy covers how information is handled.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Chrome extension Ghost Video Dial (“the Extension,” “we,” “us”) handles information. The Extension was called On the Sofa for YouTube before version 2.0.0. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, YouTube, Netflix, Amazon (Prime Video), DWANGO (niconico), or any other video service it works with.

1. Summary

  • The Extension works inside your browser. It does not send your viewing history, the pages you visit, or what you do on them to us or to anyone else. We do not run a server that receives such data.
  • It runs on YouTube from installation. On Netflix, Prime Video, and every other site it runs only after you allow that site in the Extension’s popup. You can take that permission back at any time.
  • Settings, license details, and a list of sites you used it on are kept in Chrome’s own storage (section 3).
  • The only network communication of the Extension itself is with Lemon Squeezy’s License API, to activate and check a Pro license. Buying Pro opens Lemon Squeezy’s checkout in a new tab.
  • We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.
  • When you uninstall, Chrome may open an optional feedback form (Google Forms). Answering is voluntary.

2. What the Extension does on web pages

The Extension runs on:

  • YouTube (www.youtube.com): from installation.
  • Netflix and Prime Video (Amazon’s Prime Video pages and primevideo.com): after you allow them in the popup.
  • Any other site: only a site you allow yourself, one at a time, with “Use on this site” in the popup (an experimental feature). The permission is held by Chrome; you can remove it in the popup or in Chrome’s extension settings.

On those pages, and only there, the Extension:

  • finds the video element and reads its playback state (position, length, speed, volume);
  • reads the page’s layout to place the ghost area over the video and to keep the page from scrolling under it;
  • changes the position, speed, or volume when you turn the mouse wheel over the ghost area. For this it may use the site’s own player inside the page (YouTube, Netflix) or send the site’s own keyboard shortcuts to its player (niconico), so that the site’s controls show the same values;
  • checks whether an ad is showing, to hide the ghost area during ads.

All of this happens in your browser. Nothing about the page, the video, or your use of it is sent to us. When the Extension changes playback through a site’s own controls, the site may keep that setting (for example, your preferred speed) the same way it would if you had used its controls yourself.

3. Information stored in your browser

3.1 Chrome sync storage

If Chrome Sync is on, Chrome copies this data to your other Chrome browsers signed in to the same account.

  • Preferences (wheel direction, which kinds of pages the Extension works on, whether the introduction was shown)
  • Slot settings (common, and per site where you chose them)
  • Where the ghost area sits on each site: the site names (for example “example.com”) of sites where you moved the ghost area or saved settings. At most 50 recently used sites are kept, plus at most 50 sites whose settings you saved; older ones are removed automatically.
  • License key, license instance name and ID, the date of the last license check, the expiry date, and whether Pro is active (Pro only)

3.2 Chrome local storage (this browser only)

  • The last playback speed and volume on YouTube, and the last speed on Netflix, to use them on the next video

3.3 Chrome session storage (cleared when the browser closes)

  • The last speed and volume on each site, with the site name, to carry them over to that site’s next video

None of this data is sent to us.

4. Information sent to third parties

4.1 Lemon Squeezy (Pro checkout and licenses)

If you buy Pro, the Extension opens Lemon Squeezy’s hosted checkout in a new tab. The Extension never sees or stores card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses. Lemon Squeezy, as Merchant of Record, processes what the sale needs (typically name, email address, payment method, billing and tax details, and the order record) under its Privacy Policy and Buyer Terms.

When you enter a license key, and about once a day while one is stored, the Extension sends to Lemon Squeezy’s License API (api.lemonsqueezy.com):

  • the license key;
  • the license instance name and ID (to count activations).

Lemon Squeezy’s answer includes details of the order behind the key (such as the store and product, and the buyer’s name and email address). The Extension uses only the store and product, to confirm that the key is for this Extension, and the license status and expiry date. It does not store the rest.

4.2 Sites you use the Extension on

The Extension sends nothing to these sites beyond the ordinary effect of controlling their player (section 2). Your use of them is subject to their own privacy policies, for YouTube Google’s Privacy Policy.

4.3 Uninstall feedback (optional)

When you remove the Extension, Chrome opens a feedback form hosted by Google Forms in a new tab. Answers you choose to submit are stored in Google’s systems for the developer. No viewing data is included. You may close the tab without answering.

5. Permissions and why they are used

  • storage — keep settings and license details (section 3)
  • alarms — check a Pro license about once a day
  • scripting — run the ghost area on Netflix, Prime Video, and sites you allow
  • activeTab — show the current tab’s site in the popup, so you can allow the Extension there
  • www.youtube.com — run the ghost area on YouTube
  • api.lemonsqueezy.com — activate and check Pro licenses
  • Optional: netflix.com, and websites (http / https) — requested only when you allow Netflix, Prime Video, or a particular site in the popup, and only for that site

6. Information we do not collect

  • Viewing, browsing, or search history, and the content of pages you visit
  • Payment card numbers or other payment credentials (these stay with Lemon Squeezy)
  • Personal information beyond what you give Lemon Squeezy at checkout
  • Location
  • What you type on websites

7. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the Extension’s features described here, and is not transferred, sold, or used for advertising or creditworthiness.

8. Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 (or the age that applies in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Data in Chrome storage stays until you uninstall the Extension or clear its data in Chrome. Uninstalling removes it from that browser profile. Session storage is cleared when the browser closes. Deactivating your license in the popup removes the license key from the Extension. Lemon Squeezy keeps order and license records under its own policies.

10. Users outside Japan

Lemon Squeezy may process payment and license data in other countries under its own policies and the law that applies (including tax law). The Extension itself sends no browsing or viewing data anywhere.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above changes with each update, and significant changes are also announced in the Extension’s Chrome Web Store listing or its update notes.

12. Contact

Use the support contact on the Extension’s Chrome Web Store listing, or reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt.

Terms of Sale

Ghost Video Dial — Pro
Last updated: September 30, 2026

These Terms of Sale describe the paid Pro license. They apply together with Lemon Squeezy’s Buyer Terms; for the payment itself, the checkout page and Lemon Squeezy’s terms prevail. How information is handled is in the Privacy Policy.

1. Who sells and who provides

Lemon Squeezy sells the Pro license to you as Merchant of Record: it takes the payment and handles tax, invoices, and refunds. The developer of the Extension provides the Extension and its Pro features and supports them. Google does not sell Pro.

2. What you buy

Pro unlocks:

  • every slot of the ghost area to be set freely (the free version sets the leftmost slot);
  • more slots (up to 10);
  • per-site settings for every slot.

The free version stays usable without payment. The price and currency are shown on Lemon Squeezy’s checkout before you pay; tax may be added depending on where you live.

3. One-year license (no automatic renewal)

Each purchase gives a Pro license for about one year from the purchase date (the exact end date is the one Lemon Squeezy sets on the key). It is a one-time payment: it does not renew automatically and nothing is charged again unless you complete a new checkout.

After payment Lemon Squeezy emails a license key; enter it in the Extension’s popup. When the year ends the key expires and the Extension goes back to the free version. To continue, buy a new one-year license and enter the new key. That is a new purchase, not a renewal charge.

One key can be active in up to five browsers at a time (each Chrome profile counts as one, and so may a reinstall). To use it in another browser, choose “Deactivate license” in one you no longer use. If you cannot, contact support and old activations will be cleared.

4. Stopping

  • There is no subscription to cancel and no future charge is scheduled.
  • “Deactivate license” in the popup only removes Pro from that browser.
  • Uninstalling removes local settings; it does not create a charge.
  • Unused time is not normally refunded if you stop using Pro early.

5. Refunds

Pro is a digital license. Refund requests are decided case by case by the developer and Lemon Squeezy; once a key has been issued and used, a refund is usually not available. Lemon Squeezy may refund at its discretion under its Buyer Terms. Rights you have under the consumer law of your country are not affected.

To ask for a refund, use the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing or reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt, with the order number and the email address used at checkout.

6. Licenses bought before

  • Licenses bought for On the Sofa for YouTube keep working in Ghost Video Dial.
  • One-time (lifetime) licenses sold before the one-year plan stay valid with no extra charge. They do not expire after a year.

7. Sites and services

The Extension works with video sites as they are. A site may change in a way that stops a feature from working until the Extension is updated, and use on sites other than YouTube, Netflix, and Prime Video is experimental. No particular site is guaranteed to be supported. The Extension does not bypass any site’s restrictions: for example, speeds a site offers only to its paying members stay limited as that site decides.

8. Contact

Use the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing, or your Lemon Squeezy receipt. Orders and keys are also on Lemon Squeezy’s My Orders page.

  • Netflix と プライムビデオ（Amazon のプライムビデオのページと primevideo.com）：ポップアップで許可したあと。

  • 動画の要素を見つけ、再生状態（再生位置、長さ、速度、音量）を読み取ります。
  • ゴーストエリアを動画の上に置くため、また、その下でページがスクロールしないよう、ページの配置を読み取ります。
  • ゴーストエリアの上でホイールを回したとき、再生位置・速度・音量を変えます。サイトの操作バーと同じ値を表示させるため、ページ内のサイト自身のプレーヤーを使ったり（YouTube、Netflix）、サイトのキーボードショートカットをプレーヤーに送ったり（ニコニコ）することがあります。

これらはすべてブラウザの中で行われ、ページや動画、利用の内容が当方に送られることはありません。本拡張がサイト自身の操作を通して再生を変えた場合、利用者が自分でその操作をしたときと同じように、サイトがその設定（好みの速度など）を保存することがあります。

  • ライセンスキー、ライセンスインスタンスの名前と ID、最後にライセンスを確認した日時、有効期限、Pro かどうか（Pro のみ）

3.2 Chrome のローカルストレージ（このブラウザだけ）

  • 次の動画で使うための、YouTube での最後の再生速度と音量、Netflix での最後の再生速度

3.3 Chrome のセッションストレージ（ブラウザを閉じると消えます）

  • 同じサイトの次の動画に引き継ぐための、サイトごとの最後の再生速度と音量（サイト名とともに）
